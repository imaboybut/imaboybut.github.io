Jinwoo Chung

I am an M.S. student in Computer Engineering at Kookmin University, advised by Prof. Jangho Kim. I received my B.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Kookmin University in 2025.

My research focuses on efficient and trustworthy foundation models, especially quantization and model compression for VLMs, diffusion models, video models, and low-bit deployment. Recently, I have been studying how quantization changes model behavior, including generative diversity and cross-modal attention routing.
